# Sparrow AI — product context (paste into a new chat)

*Written 20 Nov 2026. Founder: Naim (works with Claude Code; wants short, simple English, blunt pushback, no legal/safety lectures repeated).*

## One-liner
Agentic pre-sales for real estate agents. New private-owner listing → WhatsApp from the **agent's own number** within minutes → AI qualifies the owner in Spanish (also French/English) → valuation meeting booked in the **agent's Google Calendar**. Fully automated end to end until the meeting; the agent just shows up.

Business model: **we earn per result — €30 per booked meeting**. No setup fee (the old €90 fee is being dropped; the Stripe payment step is skipped when `STRIPE_PAYMENT_LINK` is empty). Meta's WhatsApp message fees are paid by the agent directly to Meta. First customers: RE/MAX independent agents (one pilot agent is French, with French-speaking clients). Website: www.sparrowai.eu (Lovable). App: app.sparrowai.eu (Railway).

## End-to-end flow
1. **Agent onboarding (one hands-on conversation, then hands-off):** a guided chat with a named AI assistant (openly an AI). Steps: Google sign-in (name, email, calendar proven by reading it once) → agency + zones → free-text "how do you want me to work" (goes into the AI prompt, never beats the hard rules) → preview of the exact first message → (payment, skipped) → WhatsApp Business check → Meta Embedded Signup (Coexistence: the agent keeps their WhatsApp Business app and Sparrow connects to the same number) → alert number → wait for Meta template approval. "Hablar con una persona" is always visible. Screens in Spanish/English/French; the owners' language is chosen in the chat (es|fr|en).
2. **Prospecting step (optional, in the same chat):** agent picks ONE zone, property types, price range, daily cap (3/5/10); **writes their own cold first message** (edit a suggested text; placeholders `{nombre} {tipo} {zona} {agente} {agencia}`); optionally adds a results line the AI may use when an owner says no; reads 4 plain-language risks and explicitly accepts responsibility; taps "Sí, actívalo".
3. **Lead intake:** from (a) CASAFARI Private Seller Leads API (new FSBO/"particular" listings with phone, Spain), (b) generic webhook/CSV intake (`/intake/{key}`, used for Lystos exports), later Lystos API. All become the same standard lead.
4. **First contact:** WhatsApp template from the agent's number. Cold leads use the agent's own template `cold_first_contact_v{n}` (submitted to Meta under the agent's WABA; nothing is pulled or sent until Meta approves). Every cold message ends with a **mandatory footer** the agent cannot remove: "Te escribe el asistente virtual de {agente}. Vi tu anuncio publicado en un portal inmobiliario. Responde BAJA para no recibir más. Más información: privacy link (#propietarios)".
5. **AI conversation:** seller script (es/fr/en), one question at a time, tutea, qualifies (still selling? motivation, timing, price expectation, agency involved?, occupied/empty), offers valuation slots, books in Google Calendar, sends reminders. Agent can take over any chat; once the agent writes, the AI stops for that lead.
6. **Objections for cold leads:** hard stops (BAJA/STOP/ARRÊT, "no me escribas más", data-deletion requests, threats) end the chat in code; data requests/threats hand off to the agent with a reason. "No quiero agencias / ya vendo yo" gets exactly ONE friendly reply with the agent's own results line (never invented numbers); a second no ends it and sets do_not_contact. "Where did you get my number?" → honest: saw your public listing on a property portal. No follow-up nagging for cold leads (only a "no reply" mark after 3 days).
7. **Result:** meeting event in the agent's calendar → billable (€30) when accepted and created; not counted if cancelled >24h ahead.

## Guardrails (code-enforced)
- Every outbound message goes through `send_to_lead` → `can_contact` (allowed hours, 24h window, do_not_contact). The LLM never writes lead state; LLM/tool error → handoff. AI always introduces itself as "el asistente virtual de {agente}" (G13). AI usage capped per lead/agent (`ai_max_*`).
- Prospecting is opt-in twice (global `PROSPECTING_ENABLED` + per-account), Spain only, live accounts only. Daily cap (agent max 20, hard max 50), ramp-up (3/day first 3 days, 5/day to day 7). Pull every 3 hours (`PROSPECTING_POLL_HOURS`).
- **Auto-pause (never auto-resumes):** Meta number quality drops (quality rating read from Meta before every scan and on Meta's quality webhook), opt-out rate >15% of last 50 cold leads (min 10), or CASAFARI out of tokens. Operator alert + agent alert. Admin page `/admin/prospecting` shows per-agent status with pause/resume.
- De-duplication: same phone, same listing, same flat on several portals, existing leads, opted-out numbers (shared opt-out list across agents, hash only).
- CASAFARI data rules: each lead stores source + expiry date; leads that never answered are deleted at expiry (default 365 days, or CASAFARI account closing + 30 days); raw payloads never kept; phones/names never printed in logs.

## Data source research (verified Nov 2026)
- **CASAFARI Private Seller Leads API — chosen.** Per docs: area (location id) + private seller + `contact.phone/name/email` + portal (Idealista, Fotocasa, Habitaclia, Pisos). Poll-only (no push/webhook; their Alerts product is withdrawn from self-serve). Cost: 10 tokens per search page (€0.04/token = €0.40 per page of up to 25-50). Plans: Free 1,000 tokens; Agent €49/mo 1,500; Agency €249/mo 10,000. Live test, Zaragoza last 30 days: 25 listings/page, **60% have a phone, ~40% usable after filters**, ≈ €0.04 per usable lead, ~6 new usable private listings/day. **France is weak** (Lyon: 1/25 with phone). Terms: store results only while the account is open + 30 days; no reselling/passing results to third parties (so one Sparrow key serving many agents is a question to ask CASAFARI; safe default = one key per agent); no AI-model training; customer is independent controller and responsible for lawful contact. CASAFARI states no portal agreements/legal basis for seller phones.
- **Lystos** (Barcelona, Next Proptech): API Developer €150/mo, Business €300/mo (adds new/price-change/withdrawn webhooks), sold separately from Explora (€79/€199 per licence). Unknown: whether API/alerts return phones or filter private sellers (ask them). Has listing history/price drops (future "stale / price-reduced listing" idea; skipped for now). Founder is NOT buying the API for now.
- Rejected: ClickExplora (€50/mo; terms ban bots, bulk extraction, feeding AI), Idealista's MCP/connector (Claude-chat only, bans automation/bulk, no phone/seller-type filter), unofficial scrapers (Apify etc.: portal-terms and database-right risk lands on Sparrow). Building our own portal scraper contradicts the "no liability" goal.
- Idea parked until PMF: pooled/shared pulls across agents (needs CASAFARI's written OK), faster-than-3h polling, bidding for hot zones, analytics on message performance.

## Legal / liability model (drafted, NOT lawyer-reviewed)
- Terms of service + privacy policy (es/en/fr, `/terminos`, `/privacidad`): agent is a business customer and the **data controller** for owners it contacts (decides whom to message, the text, the lawful basis, honours opt-outs); Sparrow is **processor** (full art. 28 DPA in the terms, sub-processors: Meta, Google, Microsoft Azure OpenAI, Railway, Stripe) and its own controller only for aggregated/pseudonymised analytics (abuse prevention, message performance). Agent indemnifies Sparrow; Sparrow liability capped at 12 months of fees; Sparrow can pause anything instantly; Spanish law. Privacy policy has a section for owners (GDPR art. 14 notice) linked from every cold message.
- Honest limits: a contract only allocates liability between Sparrow and the agent; regulators/Meta/owners can still pursue Sparrow. Main open risk: **LSSI art. 21 (Spain) / CPCE L.34-5 (France)** on unsolicited commercial WhatsApp messages; legitimate interest under GDPR does not cure it. Also: processor vs joint controller, whether fines can be indemnified, a recorded click-accept of the terms at sign-up (not built). Cheap protections: S.L. company, liability insurance, lawyer review, keep the guardrails.
