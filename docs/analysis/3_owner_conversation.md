# Sparrow AI: the owner conversation (sales-coach + conversation-design review)

Evidence: committed rehearsals `docs/rehearsal/{1,2,3}*.txt` (R1/R2/R3) plus 12 new scenarios I ran with the real model (`scratchpad/reh/out{1,2,3}.txt`, script copy `scratchpad/reh/reh.py`; fake WhatsApp, nothing sent). Rehearsal clock = Tuesday 1 Dec 10:00 Madrid.

## Verdict
The skeleton is good: short, honest, disclosed, safe, tool-gated. As a seller it is weak in three places: it never gives the owner a reason to say yes (the "estudio de mercado" is mentioned once, in the confirmation), it dodges the two questions every Spanish owner asks (price, commission), and it has bugs that silently lose or mislead leads (Saturday availability, false promises, opt-out false positives, no nudge after slots).

## Findings

### HIGH
1. **The slot tool cannot honour a day or time the owner asks for, and the model then says "no hay hueco" falsely.** `compute_free_slots` (engine.py:845-876) returns at most 6 slots, 2 per day, so only the first ~3 days are ever visible; `get_free_slots` has no parameters (llm.py:13-19). Evidence: new `dates_and_booking` ("¿el sábado por la mañana?" -> "Este sábado no tengo hueco", twice, even after the owner agreed "Vale el sábado" + gave the address); R2/R3 `es_voice_and_odd` ("El sábado no aparece hueco"); `call_me` ("a las 11" -> "no me aparece libre", though 11:00 is inside the 10:00-13:30 window and free, it just isn't on the 3h-spaced grid). Saturday exists in `slot_days` and the horizon. Fix: `get_free_slots(date_from, date_to, part_of_day, near_time)`, return up to 12, and tell the model it may only say "no hay hueco" if a query for that day returned empty.
2. **No chase after the owner replies.** `job_followup` returns if `has_replied`, and `handle_inbound` cancels followups on any reply (engine.py:399-401, 582). An owner who says "sí" and gets two slots, then goes quiet (the commonest drop-off), is never nudged; once 24h pass only templates are possible. Fix: `slot_nudge` job 4h after slots were offered (free text, inside window: "¿Te encaja alguno de los dos huecos? Si prefieres otro día, dime cuál"), a last template at day 2-3 using FU2 wording below.
3. **The AI promises contact the system will never make.** `not_now` ("igual en primavera"): "cuando se acerque primavera te escribo", repeated in the next turn. No job exists; lead sits in `in_conversation` forever. Fix: add a `snooze(days)` tool that schedules one re-contact template, or forbid the promise and close with "cuando quieras, escríbeme".
4. **Handoff on the first commission/anger signal kills the lead and is tone-deaf.** `commission_statement` ("os lleváis un 5% y 6%... un robo") -> instant handoff; R2 `es_commission`, `es_price_haggle`; `legal_heirs` (inheritance with a disputing sibling) -> "Perfecto, se lo paso a Laura". The fixed text `handoff_to_lead` (lang.py:85) always starts with "Perfecto" and promises "en breve" with no SLA; handoff sets `human_owned` forever. If Laura is on a visit or it is Sunday night, the owner waits, the 24h window closes, nothing happens. Fix: objection ladder before handoff (rewrite below), 4 context-specific handoff texts, an "agent didn't answer in 3h" re-alert, and a "Laura te escribe hoy / mañana a primera hora" based on clock.
5. **Opt-out false positives destroy live leads.** `is_opt_out_text` (guards.py:132-155) matches `no me (llam|contact|escrib|moleste)\w*` with no qualifier check. Verified: "no me llames por la mañana, prefiero por la tarde" and "no me molestéis en horario de trabajo, escribidme por la tarde" return True -> permanent `do_not_contact` + goodbye. Fix: only treat as opt-out if the message has no time/preference clause (`por la mañana|por la tarde|en horario|hasta|antes de|después`), or send those to the model.
6. **First message claims "Vi tu anuncio" for every lead, and the source answer is incoherent.** Template v2 body, `lead_first_contact_v2` (templates.py:44). Intake source is "lystos", so R1-R3 `es_who_are_you` literally say "Laura vio tu anuncio en Lystos" (the owner has no idea what Lystos is), and for a form lead "vi tu anuncio" is untrue. Zone falls back to "tu zona" ("Vi tu anuncio en tu zona"). Also "puedo prepararte una valoración" is the assistant claiming a human's work. Fix: neutral hook (below) plus a `source_sentence` per source type ("dejaste tus datos pidiendo una valoración de tu vivienda por internet" vs "vio tu anuncio publicado en un portal"), confirmed with Lystos. `where_number_portal`: the answer then immediately re-pitches ("si te va bien, Laura puede hacerte una valoración") to a person who just wrote "esto es spam"; it must apologise, state the source, give BAJA, and stop selling.

### MED
7. **Direct "are you a robot?" is dodged.** R2/R3: "Soy el asistente virtual de Laura; ella está detrás y revisa la conversación." Never says "sí, soy una IA" and claims Laura "revisa todo" (unverifiable; the prompt only says she is "detrás"). Fix: "Sí, soy una inteligencia artificial. Laura es quien te atiende en persona." Drop "revisa".
8. **Over-eager and repetitive slots.** `just_curious` ("solo curiosidad, no tengo prisa") -> immediate "Laura puede pasar mañana a las 10:00"; `saturday_evening` ("cualquier día me va bien") -> same two slots repeated verbatim; R2 `es_voice_and_odd` ("ok" -> same slots twice); R3 `es_normal` re-offers slots in 4 of 6 replies. Slots are same-day with 2h notice ("hoy 12:30 o hoy 16:30", R2): a stranger's visit in two hours. Prompt says "como mucho 2 preguntas y pasa a los huecos", which the model reads as "skip rapport". Fix: one qualifying question min (motivo/plazo), never two slots on the same day, visit >= next day unless owner says "hoy", on "cualquier día" propose ONE slot.
9. **Price and commission answers are canned deflections.** "El valor exacto te lo da Laura en la visita, tras ver la vivienda" appears in ES and FR in most transcripts, almost verbatim. Correct in substance (no quoting) but it gives nothing. Give the reason and the deliverable (see rewrite 2). `commission_statement` shows the model, when it doesn't hand off, produces non-sequiturs ("precisamente por eso Laura puede darte una orientación", R3).
10. **Reactions/media trigger AI turns.** Non-text types become "[reaction]" / "[image]" (engine.py:572-573). A thumbs-up reaction to the first template starts an AI turn, sets `has_replied` and cancels both follow-ups. Treat reaction as a log-only event; for image/document without caption reply with a fixed one-liner or ignore.
11. **Reschedule/cancel in chat = handoff** (prompt + `do_handoff`). It is the most frequent post-booking event and breaks the "hands-off" promise; `dates_and_booking` also showed the model handing off a "move it" request when nothing was booked, after the owner believed Saturday was agreed. Add `reschedule_slot` (reuse `book_slot` lock + calendar update) and `cancel_meeting`.
12. **Hours gate delays inbound leads.** First contact held to 09-21 Mon-Sat (guards.py:68-71): a Friday 21:30 request is answered Saturday 09:00; Sunday -> Monday. For leads that asked (solicited), speed is the product ("30 s"). Keep 09-21 for cold; use 08:00-22:00 incl. Sunday for inbound (still marketing template).
13. **Booking promise not delivered.** Confirmation says "Laura te llevará un estudio de precios de tu zona" (prompt.py:292) but nothing tells Laura (calendar description `event_description` has no such line). Add "Compromiso al lead: estudio de precios de la zona" to the event. Also the AI-written confirmation is not the approved `meeting_confirmation_v1` (dead template, templates.py:87); fine inside the window, but then drop the template.
14. **Follow-up texts presuppose consent.** FU1 "¿Te viene mejor esta semana o la próxima?" and FU2 "¿sigue en pie lo de valorar tu vivienda?" imply the owner agreed. Day-1 send at 10:00 reaches people at work; evening (18-20h) is better for Spanish owners. Rewrite below.
15. **Tú/usted not mirrored.** Prompt hard-codes tú for ES (and vous for FR). Older heirs/owners (65+) often answer "Buenos días, ..." in usted. Add: mirror the owner's register.

### LOW
16. Call reminder grammar: 24h template reads "Laura pasa por el teléfono (te llamará)" (lang.py:68-70); create call-specific reminder templates.
17. No no-show or post-meeting step (no handler for `Meeting` after start). Needed for BL-3; at minimum a 2h-after alert to the agent ("¿se celebró?") and a recovery text on "no".
18. `_PROMISE_RE` (coldmsg.py:59) warns the agent on "gratis/gratuit/free", discouraging the one hook that works. The cold default body has no hook at all ("¿Sigues vendiéndolo por tu cuenta?").
19. FR date "mardi 1 décembre" should be "1er" (lang.py:52-54). EN/FR first messages and objection replies read natively; FR `je suis l'assistant` gender fixed.
20. Voice notes: only works when Azure transcription is configured; otherwise "¿Me lo puedes escribir?" (add "o si prefieres, Laura te llama"). Catalan/English handled well (R: `catalan_and_english`), but the model re-asks the same condition question in the new language.
21. "Lo di de baja" (listing withdrawn) also trips opt-out (verified).

## What works (keep)
G13 intro in the template and in every language; hard-stop code before the model (BAJA, data requests, threats; R2 `es_hostile`); "no" after FU2 treated as opt-out; owner-is-agent and buyer routed sensibly; exclusive-agency handling (R3: thanks, does not interfere, one offer, then stops); Catalan reply; burst grouping; length mostly 1-2 sentences; one "✅" only in confirmation.

## Three highest-impact rewrites (Spanish)

**A. First message (template `lead_first_contact_v3`, MARKETING; fixes #6, hook, honesty)**
> Hola {{1}}, soy el asistente virtual de {{2}} ({{3}}). Te escribo por tu vivienda en {{4}}: {{2}} puede prepararte, sin coste ni compromiso, un estudio de mercado con los precios a los que se han vendido pisos parecidos por tu zona. ¿Sigues pensando en venderla?
>
> Si prefieres que no te escriba más, responde BAJA.

(The condition question moves to turn 2; "¿sigues pensando en venderla?" is a yes/no an owner answers in two seconds and it qualifies intent.)
FU1: "Hola {{1}}, por si se te pasó mi mensaje: {{2}} puede enseñarte a cuánto se están vendiendo pisos como el tuyo en {{3}}, sin compromiso. ¿Te lo preparo?" FU2: "Hola {{1}}, no quiero molestarte: si ya no te interesa, responde NO y no te escribo más. Si aún lo estás valorando, dímelo y Laura te llama cuando te venga bien."

**B. Objections block (replace prompt.py:304 and the commission handoff rule, line 302)**
> - Comisión/"es un robo"/"lo vendo yo solo": primero valida en una frase ("Lo entiendo, es una decisión importante y es normal mirar el coste."), luego una frase de valor SIN cifras ("{agent_first} te enseña con ventas reales de tu zona qué precio de salida funciona y cuánto tardan en venderse, y tú decides si te compensa; la valoración no cuesta nada.") y UNA pregunta ("¿Lo estás anunciando tú ahora mismo?"). Solo haz handoff si pide la cifra por segunda vez, o si se enfada de verdad.
> - "Solo curiosidad / cuánto vale": "Sin ver la vivienda cualquier cifra sería inventada. Lo que sí hace {agent_first} es un estudio con lo que se ha vendido de verdad cerca de ti: sin compromiso y sin prisa. ¿Prefieres que te lo cuente por llamada de 10 minutos o verlo en persona?"
> - "Ahora no": no prometas escribir. Di "Sin problema, cuando quieras me escribes por aquí" y usa snooze(días) si hay fecha.
> - ¿Eres un robot?: "Sí, soy una inteligencia artificial, el asistente virtual de {agent_first}. Ella es quien te atenderá en persona."

**C. Slot rules (replace prompt.py:288-292)**
> - Antes de ofrecer huecos haz UNA pregunta de contexto (motivo o plazo) salvo que ya pida cita. Si dice "solo curiosidad", ofrece primero la llamada corta.
> - Ofrece 2 huecos en días distintos, nunca los dos el mismo día, y visita desde mañana salvo que pida "hoy". Si pide un día u hora concretos, llama a get_free_slots con ese día; solo di que no hay hueco si esa consulta vuelve vacía. Si dice "cualquier día", propón UN hueco ("¿El lunes 7 a las 10:00?"). Nunca repitas los mismos huecos.

## Measuring quality
Log per AI turn (LlmTurn already stores transcript): n_sentences, n_questions (flag >1), slots offered (ids) and **repeat-offer flag**, reply-language == owner-language, handoff reason category, tool-vs-text mismatch (text promises "te escribo/te llamo" without a job), "no hay hueco" claims vs tool result. Funnel by owner message index: reply rate, owner answered Q1, slots offered -> chosen -> address -> booked -> held (agent-confirmed), silence-after-slots (target <30%), opt-out attributed to the last AI message, handoff-to-agent-reply latency, time-to-first-touch for inbound (p50/p95). Weekly: LLM-judge (separate model) scores 20 random transcripts on 5 yes/no items: answered the owner's last question, one question only, no invented fact, no pressure, honest about AI.
